TEKS / WACANA PENGANTAR SOAL
28. Read the four short imaginative story drafts written by Class 8A students below: Draft 1: Lutfi loves cats. Cats have soft fur and four legs. Yesterday he bought cat food at the market. Draft 2: "Give me back my pen!" shouted Reno angrily in the classroom, and then the bell rang loudly. Draft 3: One day, Sari lost her dictionary. She looked everywhere in the classroom and under the teacher's desk, but she never found it until she graduated. Draft 4: On a rainy Friday afternoon in the school library, generous Kiki saw her friend Arin looking anxious because Arin's umbrella was broken. "Come share my umbrella, Arin; I'll walk you to your house first!" Kiki offered warmly, and they walked home safely under one roof. Which draft successfully includes all required narrative elements (a clear beginning, a problem, a kind-hearted solution, emotional adjectives, and direct speech)?
